description: Propose 3 distinct, named design directions before building
argument-hint: <project or brief>

Using `god-of-design` and `god-styles`, propose **three clearly different** aesthetic directions for: $ARGUMENTS

For each direction give: a name, a one-sentence concept, the style IDs it draws from, a palette (hex with roles), a font pair (Google Fonts), a layout idea, one signature detail, and the risk. Then recommend one and wait for the user's choice before building.
