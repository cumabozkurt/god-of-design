## Pairing rules

- **Contrast of structure:** serif + sans, or geometric + humanist. Not two neo-grotesques.
- **Similar x-height** helps mixed lines sit together.
- **Shared mood:** era and personality must agree (don't pair a Victorian display face with a futurist body face unless that is the concept).
- **Weights:** display 600–900, body 400 (use 450 if available on dark backgrounds), labels 500.
