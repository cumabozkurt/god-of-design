# Prompt Library (adapt subject, palette and aspect ratio)

## Web & UI
- **Hero illustration:** `editorial conceptual illustration of [idea], flat shapes with subtle grain, limited palette of [hex1] [hex2] [hex3], generous negative space on the left for headline, 16:9`
- **Product mockup:** `premium studio product photo of [product] on travertine pedestal, soft diffused light, long gentle shadow, neutral beige background, 4:5`
- **App reference board:** `mobile app UI mood board for a [category] app in [style] style, 6 screens arranged in a grid, consistent design system, high fidelity`
- **Abstract background:** `soft aurora mesh gradient in [hex colours], fine film grain, no objects, seamless, 16:9`

## Social media
- **Quote card background:** `minimal textured paper background in [colour], subtle shadow of a plant from the top right, empty centre for text, 4:5`
- **Event poster art:** `[style] poster artwork for a [event], bold central motif of [object], dramatic composition, space at top for title, 4:5`
- **YouTube thumbnail photo:** `expressive close-up of a person reacting with surprise, looking at [object] on the right, bright rim light, saturated background in [colour], 16:9`

## Branding
- **Logo exploration (to redraw as vector, never ship the raw output):** `minimal geometric logo mark for [brand], combining [concept A] and [concept B], flat single colour on white, centred, vector style`
- **Brand imagery style:** `lifestyle photography for [brand], natural light, authentic candid moments, colour grade with warm highlights and teal shadows, 35mm film look`
- **Pattern:** `seamless repeat pattern inspired by [tradition, e.g. Anatolian kilim motifs], [palette], flat textile print, tileable`

## Print
- **Book cover art:** `book cover illustration for a [genre] novel about [theme], [technique], strong single focal image, space for title at top third, 2:3`
- **Packaging illustration:** `botanical illustration of [ingredient], engraving style with delicate cross-hatching, cream background, for packaging label`

## Presentations
- **Section image:** `wide cinematic photo representing [concept], minimal composition, muted colour palette in [colours], plenty of negative space on the right, 16:9`

## Cultural styles (use respectfully)
- **İznik:** `Ottoman İznik ceramic tile, cobalt blue and turquoise tulips and carnations on white, red bole accents, symmetrical, high detail`
- **Ukiyo-e landscape:** `ukiyo-e woodblock print of [modern subject], Hiroshige style composition, Prussian blue bokashi sky, flat colour, key-block outlines`
- **Islamic geometric:** `precise Islamic geometric 10-fold star pattern, girih tiles, lapis and gold on cream plaster, flat, symmetrical`
- **Madhubani-inspired (credit artists in commercial use):** `folk painting inspired by Madhubani, double-line outlines, fish and peacocks, natural pigments`

## Negative prompt starters (SD/FLUX-style)
`blurry, low resolution, extra fingers, deformed hands, watermark, signature, text artifacts, jpeg artifacts, oversaturated, plastic skin, generic stock photo, cluttered background`
