# UI Microcopy

- **Be specific.** "Save changes" > "Submit". "Delete 3 files" > "Confirm".
- **Front-load keywords.** Users scan the first 2 words.
- **Errors:** what happened + how to fix it, without blame. "Card declined. Try another card or contact your bank." Not "Error 402" or "Oops! Something went wrong 😢".
- **Empty states:** explain the value and offer the next step. "No invoices yet. Create your first invoice to get paid faster."
- **Buttons:** verb + object, sentence case. Keep labels consistent across the flow.
- **Avoid AI-sounding filler:** "Unlock", "Elevate", "Seamless", "Revolutionize", "Supercharge", "Effortlessly", "In today's fast-paced world", "Whether you're X or Y". Say the concrete thing.
- **Numbers over adjectives:** "Set up in 4 minutes" > "Quick setup".
- **Localisation-ready:** no text in images, allow +30–40% length (German, Turkish, Finnish), don't concatenate strings, use ICU plural rules.
- **Turkish UI notes:** address users with *siz* in formal products or *sen* in casual brands, and stay consistent. Use Turkish quotes and punctuation conventions, and suffixes after proper nouns with an apostrophe ("GitHub'a", "Cuma'nın").
